Gas Raft Rapids

Two to four of you share one raft. The only way to steer is to fart. Back seats push the raft forward, front seats brake, and farting from one side swings the raft toward the other. The secret is farting together, at the right moment.

Play on this device

Couch game, one keyboard: A back left, L back right, Q front left, P front right. On a phone, each player holds their own part of the screen.

Solo, you work both back seats: A or ← farts left, L or → farts right. Hold both to go straight. On a phone, hold the left or right half of the screen.
