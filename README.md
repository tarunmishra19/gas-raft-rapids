# Gas Raft Rapids

A 2–4 player co-op raft game. Everyone shares one raft and steers it by farting together. Open the page, host a game, send friends the link and the four-letter code.

## Courses

Pick a course on the title screen. Finishing one carries the crew on to the next.

1. **Lazy River** — a gentle warm-up with a couple of narrow pinches.
2. **Toot Canyon** — sharper turns, two logs and a whirlpool in a wide pool.
3. **The Big Squeeze** — a long squeeze, then a slalom of rocks.
4. **Duck Bay** — a wide, sunny lagoon where rows of ducks paddle across the channel.
5. **Frostbottom Fjord** — ice water with almost no drag, so the raft keeps sliding into every bend.
6. **Stinky Sewer** — a tight zigzag tunnel with pillars in the channel and fast currents down the drops.
7. **Volcano Vent** — hot springs between lava rocks; geysers on the banks blast the raft across the channel.
8. **Gas Gauntlet** — the long finale with everything at once.
